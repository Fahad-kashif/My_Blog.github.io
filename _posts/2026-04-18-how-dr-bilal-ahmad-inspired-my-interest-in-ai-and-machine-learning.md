# 🤖 How Dr. Bilal Ahmad Inspired My Interest in AI and Machine Learning

When I joined UET Lahore Faisalabad Campus as a Computer Engineering student, I knew that technology was changing the world rapidly, but I had only a limited understanding of emerging fields such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL). Like many students in the early stages of their academic journey, I was focused on learning programming, completing assignments, and understanding the fundamentals of engineering. However, as my studies progressed, my perspective began to change, and a significant part of that change was influenced by Dr. Bilal Ahmad.

## Discovering a Field Beyond Traditional Programming

During my second semester, I studied Database Systems under Dr. Bilal Ahmad. Although the course itself focused on database concepts, many classroom discussions extended beyond the syllabus. Instead of treating technology as a collection of isolated subjects, he often explained how different areas of Computer Science connect with one another.

What stood out to me was the way he discussed Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Rather than presenting them as futuristic buzzwords, he described them as practical technologies that are already solving real-world problems. These discussions made me curious about the possibilities of AI and encouraged me to learn more about the field.

As students, we often focus on passing examinations and completing coursework. However, these conversations helped me think beyond semester grades and consider how technology can be applied to address challenges in healthcare, business, education, and other industries.

## Learning the Importance of Real-World Problems

One lesson that I repeatedly heard from Dr. Bilal Ahmad was the importance of solving meaningful problems. He often emphasized that technology should create value and contribute positively to society.

This idea changed the way I viewed technical projects. Instead of seeing programming as an academic requirement, I began viewing it as a tool that could potentially improve people's lives. Whether the problem involves healthcare, data analysis, or automation, technology becomes more valuable when it is connected to real-world needs.

This perspective encouraged me to think more carefully about the types of projects I wanted to pursue and the skills I wanted to develop throughout my degree.

## Understanding the Role of Data

Another important lesson I learned was the significance of data in modern technology. Before university, I rarely thought about where data comes from or how it is used. Through discussions about Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, I realized that data is one of the most valuable resources in today's digital world.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad frequently highlighted the importance of working with high-quality datasets. He explained that the success of Machine Learning systems depends heavily on the quality, accuracy, and reliability of the data used during training.

This insight helped me understand why data collection, organization, and management are critical components of any successful AI project. It also reinforced many of the concepts I was learning in Database Systems regarding structured and reliable data management.

## Why Healthcare Applications Caught My Attention

Among the many examples discussed during the semester, healthcare applications were particularly interesting to me. Medical datasets often contain valuable information that can help researchers and engineers develop systems capable of assisting healthcare professionals.

I found it fascinating that Machine Learning can be used to identify patterns in medical data, support diagnosis, and contribute to better decision-making. These examples demonstrated how technology can have a direct impact on people's well-being.

The more I learned about these applications, the more interested I became in exploring Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence as potential areas of study and future development.

## Looking Beyond the Classroom

One aspect I appreciated was the encouragement to continue learning independently. Technology evolves rapidly, and classroom lectures alone cannot cover every new development.

This motivated me to begin reading about Machine Learning concepts, AI applications, and emerging technologies outside of my regular coursework. Even though I was still a beginner, I realized that continuous learning is essential for anyone pursuing a career in technology.

The experience taught me that university education provides a foundation, but personal curiosity and self-learning are equally important for long-term growth.

## Developing a New Academic Interest

As my understanding of AI and Machine Learning grew, so did my interest in these fields. I became increasingly interested in how algorithms learn from data and how intelligent systems can assist humans in solving complex problems.

What started as simple curiosity gradually developed into a genuine academic interest. I began paying closer attention to technological advancements and exploring how Machine Learning is being applied across different industries.

This shift in perspective helped me recognize that Artificial Intelligence is not simply another subject area. It is a field that combines programming, mathematics, data analysis, and problem solving to create innovative solutions.

## The Value of Inspiration in Education

One thing I have learned during my university journey is that education is influenced not only by textbooks and lectures but also by the people who guide students along the way. A teacher's ability to connect concepts with real-world applications can significantly affect a student's interests and future goals.

For me, the discussions and insights shared by Dr. Bilal Ahmad helped transform Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from unfamiliar terms into subjects that I genuinely wanted to explore. His emphasis on practical applications, meaningful projects, and continuous learning encouraged me to think more broadly about technology and its impact on society.

## Looking Toward the Future

As I continue my Computer Engineering studies, I hope to deepen my understanding of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and related technologies. These fields are growing rapidly and are expected to play an increasingly important role in shaping the future.

The lessons I have learned so far have shown me that success in technology requires more than technical skills alone. It requires curiosity, dedication, problem-solving ability, and a willingness to continue learning.

Looking back, I am grateful that my academic journey introduced me to ideas that extended beyond the classroom. The inspiration I gained has motivated me to explore new opportunities, develop valuable skills, and continue building knowledge in areas that have the potential to create meaningful impact in the real world.
